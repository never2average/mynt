# Licence-type research gaps (India)

The repository has no per-regulator data files. Regulators and licence types are entered through `define_regulator` and stored by the running server. This note collects public research on five licence types that are not modelled yet, so they can be entered that way.

Every fact below cites its source inline. A fact without a source has been left out. Most sources are regulator or government sites. Where those blocked automated access, a copy that reproduces the official text is used, and the entry names it as a secondary source.

## 1. Third Party Administrator (TPA), health services

- **Regulator:** Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI).
- **Governing regulation:** IRDAI (Third Party Administrators – Health Services) Regulations, 2016. [policyholder.gov.in (IRDAI)](https://policyholder.gov.in/web/guest/requirements-for-license-as-a-tpa)
- **Who may hold it:** only a company with a share capital, registered under the Companies Act, 2013. Its main and sole object must be providing health services, and it may not carry on any other business. [policyholder.gov.in](https://policyholder.gov.in/web/guest/requirements-for-license-as-a-tpa)
- **Capital:** minimum paid-up equity share capital of Rs 4 crore. [policyholder.gov.in](https://policyholder.gov.in/web/guest/requirements-for-license-as-a-tpa)
- **Net worth:** must not fall below Rs 1 crore at any time during registration. [policyholder.gov.in](https://policyholder.gov.in/web/guest/requirements-for-license-as-a-tpa)
- **Foreign investment:** must comply with Government of India FDI policy and IRDAI rules. [policyholder.gov.in](https://policyholder.gov.in/web/guest/requirements-for-license-as-a-tpa)
- **Ongoing obligations:** pay an annual fee of Rs 50,000 on or before 31 January. [policyholder.gov.in](https://policyholder.gov.in/web/guest/requirements-for-license-as-a-tpa)

## 2. Self-Certified Syndicate Bank (SCSB)

- **Regulator:** Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).
- **Governing law:**
  - SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018. They define ASBA as an application for a public or rights issue that authorises a self-certified syndicate bank to block the application money in a bank account. [ICDR text, thc.nic.in (government copy)](https://thc.nic.in/Central%20Governmental%20Regulations/Securities%20and%20Exhange%20Board%20of%20India%20(%20Issue%20of%20Capital%20and%20Disclosure%20Requirements)%20Regulations,2018.pdf)
  - SEBI circular SEBI/CFD/DIL/ASBA/1/2009/30/12, 30 December 2009. [text reproduced on taxguru.in (secondary)](https://taxguru.in/sebi/sebi-applications-supported-blocked-amount-asba-facility-public-issues-rights-issues.html)
- **Who may hold it** (2009 circular, Annexure I) [taxguru.in](https://taxguru.in/sebi/sebi-applications-supported-blocked-amount-asba-facility-public-issues-rights-issues.html):
  - The bank holds a valid certificate of registration as a Banker to an Issue under the SEBI (Bankers to an Issue) Regulations, 1994.
  - It has an arrangement with at least one stock exchange's electronic bidding system.
  - It has completed a mock trial run with the exchanges and registrars.
  - It has submitted a self-certificate to SEBI and been included in SEBI's list of SCSBs.
- **Capital:** the sources give no SCSB-specific figure.
- **Ongoing obligations** (same circular) [taxguru.in](https://taxguru.in/sebi/sebi-applications-supported-blocked-amount-asba-facility-public-issues-rights-issues.html):
  - Name Designated Branches, a Controlling Branch and a senior nodal officer.
  - Upload an application only after the money is blocked, and only once.
  - Accept no more than five applications per account per issue.
  - Send aggregate blocked-amount data to the registrar by T+1.
  - Unblock or transfer funds on the registrar's instruction.
  - Keep physical ASBAs for six months and electronic records for at least three years.
  - Redress investor complaints about data capture, blocking or unblocking errors.

## 3. Commercial Paper Issuing and Paying Agent (IPA)

- **Regulator:** Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
- **Governing direction:** Master Direction – Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Paper and Non-Convertible Debentures of original or initial maturity up to one year) Directions, 2024. Notification FMRD.DIRD.10/14.02.001/2023-24 (RBI/FMRD/2023-24/109), dated 3 January 2024 and in force from 1 April 2024. It superseded the CP Directions, 2017. [rbi.org.in](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12592)
- **Who may hold it:** a Scheduled Commercial Bank. The Directions define an IPA (para 2(a)(14)) as a Scheduled Commercial Bank undertaking the duties in para 7(a). [rbi.org.in](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12592)
- **Capital:** the Directions set no IPA-specific figure.
- **Ongoing obligations** [rbi.org.in](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12592):
  - Para 7(a):
    - Ensure the issuer is authorised to borrow through CPs and NCDs.
    - Verify the issuance documents and hold them in custody.
    - Issue an IPA certificate and make it available electronically on the depositories' websites.
    - Obtain the use-of-proceeds certificate from the issuer's CEO or CFO.
    - Ensure reporting is done.
  - Para 6: report on F-TRAC by 5:30 PM on the day of each primary issuance, buyback, default, or repayment of a defaulted obligation.
  - RBI may bar an entity that breaches the Directions from acting as an IPA.

## 4. Credit Information Company (CIC)

- **Regulator:** Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
- **Governing law:** Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act, 2005 (Act 30 of 2005). [Act text, ifsca.gov.in](https://www.ifsca.gov.in/Document/Legal/71-the-credit-information-companies-regulation-act-2005-12092020034712.pdf), [indiacode.nic.in](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2057)
  - s. 3: no company may carry on the business of credit information without an RBI certificate of registration.
  - s. 4: application for registration.
  - s. 5: grant of the certificate.
  - s. 6: cancellation.
  - s. 15: every credit institution must become a member of at least one CIC.
  - s. 21: alteration of credit information files and credit reports.
- **Who may hold it:** a company that RBI registers under s. 5 of the Act. Among other conditions, s. 5(1)(a) requires it to have the minimum capital structure in s. 8. [ifsca.gov.in](https://www.ifsca.gov.in/Document/Legal/71-the-credit-information-companies-regulation-act-2005-12092020034712.pdf)
- **Capital (s. 8)** [ifsca.gov.in](https://www.ifsca.gov.in/Document/Legal/71-the-credit-information-companies-regulation-act-2005-12092020034712.pdf):
  - Authorised capital of at least Rs 30 crore, which RBI may raise by notification to up to Rs 50 crore.
  - Issued capital of at least Rs 20 crore.
  - Paid-up capital at all times of at least 75% of issued capital.
- **Current RBI directions:** Reserve Bank of India (Credit Information Companies) Directions, 2025, updated as on 1 July 2026. [rbi.org.in](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12926)
- **Ongoing obligations:** if a complaint is not resolved within 30 calendar days, the complainant gets Rs 100 per day in compensation. The credit institution has 21 days and the CIC 9 days. Source: RBI circular RBI/2023-24/72, 26 October 2023. [circular copy, pdicai.org (secondary)](https://www.pdicai.org/Docs/RBI-2023-24-72_3010202312055997.PDF)

## 5. Credit Rating Agency (CRA)

- **Regulator:** Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).
- **Governing regulation:** SEBI (Credit Rating Agencies) Regulations, 1999, as amended. One amendment is SEBI (Credit Rating Agencies) (Amendment) Regulations, 2018, notification SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2018-15 of 30 May 2018, made under s. 30 read with s. 11 of the SEBI Act, 1992. [sebi.gov.in notice](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/regulations/may-2018/securities-and-exchange-board-of-india-credit-rating-agencies-amendment-regulations-2018_39183.html), [amendment text, oss-data-in.vaquill.ai (secondary copy)](https://oss-data-in.vaquill.ai/legislation/REG_SEBI_sebiregulations39183securitiesandexchang/act.pdf)
- **Who may hold it:** a CRA must be promoted by one of the following (regulation 4):
  - a public financial institution;
  - a scheduled commercial bank;
  - a foreign bank operating in India with RBI approval;
  - a foreign CRA with at least five years' rating experience, which since 2018 must be incorporated in a FATF member jurisdiction;
  - a company or body corporate with a continuous net worth of at least Rs 100 crore.

  Sources: [sebi.gov.in](https://www.sebi.gov.in/acts/CreditRatingAgencies.html), [2018 amendment](https://oss-data-in.vaquill.ai/legislation/REG_SEBI_sebiregulations39183securitiesandexchang/act.pdf)
- **Capital** (2018 amendment) [amendment text](https://oss-data-in.vaquill.ai/legislation/REG_SEBI_sebiregulations39183securitiesandexchang/act.pdf):
  - Minimum net worth of Rs 25 crore to register (regulation 5(c)).
  - Rs 25 crore maintained at all times (regulation 9(d)). Existing CRAs had three years to comply.
  - The promoter holds at least 26% (regulation 5(l)) and keeps it for at least three years from registration (regulation 9(e)).
- **Ongoing obligations:**
  - Follow the Code of Conduct in the Third Schedule (regulation 13). [sebi.gov.in](https://www.sebi.gov.in/acts/CreditRatingAgencies.html)
  - Take all rating decisions in professional rating committees (regulation 24). [sebi.gov.in](https://www.sebi.gov.in/acts/CreditRatingAgencies.html)
  - Monitor ratings continuously for the life of the rated securities (regulation 15(1)) and review all published ratings periodically (regulation 16(1)). [amendment text](https://oss-data-in.vaquill.ai/legislation/REG_SEBI_sebiregulations39183securitiesandexchang/act.pdf)
  - Carry out no activity other than rating securities offered by public or rights issue, except as a financial sector regulator requires (regulation 9(f)). [amendment text](https://oss-data-in.vaquill.ai/legislation/REG_SEBI_sebiregulations39183securitiesandexchang/act.pdf)
  - The certificate's conditions and validity are set by regulation 9. [sebi.gov.in](https://www.sebi.gov.in/acts/CreditRatingAgencies.html)
