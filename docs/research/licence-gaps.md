# Licence-type research gaps (India)

The repository has no per-regulator data files. Regulators and licence types are entered through `define_regulator` and stored by the running server. This note collects public research on five licence types that are not modelled yet, so they can be entered that way.

Facts marked **(unverified)** come from general knowledge of the regulation. Check them against the primary text before entering them.

## 1. Third Party Administrator (TPA), health services

- **Regulator:** Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI).
- **Law:** Insurance Act, 1938, s. 42D (registration of intermediaries) (unverified section); IRDAI (Third Party Administrators – Health Services) Regulations, 2016, since folded into IRDAI's 2024 intermediaries framework (unverified).
- **Who may hold it:** a company under the Companies Act, 2013 whose sole object is TPA health services. An insurer may not hold it (unverified).
- **Capital:** minimum paid-up equity of Rs 4 crore, and a net worth of at least Rs 1 crore at all times (unverified).
- **Ongoing obligations:** act only under an agreement with an insurer; meet claims-settlement turnaround norms; protect policyholder data; file audited annual returns with IRDAI; charge no fee directly to policyholders (unverified).
- **Sources:** https://irdai.gov.in (Regulations section; no stable deep link found).

## 2. Self-Certified Syndicate Bank (SCSB)

- **Regulator:** Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).
- **Law:** SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, read with SEBI (Bankers to an Issue) Regulations, 1994 and the ASBA circulars (unverified section numbers).
- **Who may hold it:** a bank registered with SEBI as a Banker to an Issue that self-certifies it meets SEBI's ASBA conditions. SEBI publishes the list of recognised SCSBs (unverified).
- **Capital:** no SCSB-specific requirement beyond Banker-to-an-Issue registration and banking-licence capital (unverified).
- **Ongoing obligations:** block and unblock application money (ASBA, including UPI mandates); run designated branches; handle investor grievances and pay compensation within set timelines; reconcile with registrars (unverified).
- **Sources:** https://www.sebi.gov.in (Intermediaries > Self Certified Syndicate Banks).

## 3. Commercial Paper Issuing and Paying Agent (IPA)

- **Regulator:** Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
- **Law:** Reserve Bank (Commercial Paper and Non-Convertible Debentures of original or initial maturity up to one year) Directions, 2024, which replaced the 2017 CP Directions. Issued under the RBI Act, 1934, ss. 45J, 45K and 45L (unverified section list).
- **Who may hold it:** a scheduled bank acting as IPA for the CP issue (unverified).
- **Capital:** no separate requirement; bank capital norms apply (unverified).
- **Ongoing obligations:** check the issuer's documents and credit rating and certify them; report each issue to the platform RBI specifies; handle redemption and payment; report defaults (unverified).
- **Sources:** https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx (Master Directions index; the specific direction's id was not confirmed).

## 4. Credit Information Company (CIC)

- **Regulator:** Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
- **Law:** Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act, 2005: s. 5 (certificate of registration), s. 6 (requirements), s. 15 (credit institutions' membership), s. 21 (alteration of credit information); Credit Information Companies Regulations, 2006 and Rules, 2006 (unverified section mapping).
- **Who may hold it:** a company under the Companies Act that RBI has registered as a CIC.
- **Capital:** minimum capital set under the Act; Rs 100 crore (unverified).
- **Ongoing obligations:** keep data accurate and private; give each individual one free full credit report a year; resolve disputes within statutory timelines; report to RBI; come under the RBI Ombudsman (unverified).
- **Sources:** https://www.indiacode.nic.in (text of the Act; the site didn't respond when this was written), https://www.rbi.org.in.

## 5. Credit Rating Agency (CRA)

- **Regulator:** Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).
- **Law:** SEBI (Credit Rating Agencies) Regulations, 1999, made under the SEBI Act, 1992, s. 30, with registration under s. 12 of that Act.
- **Who may hold it:** a company registered with SEBI and promoted as the Regulations require, for example by a public financial institution, a scheduled bank, a foreign bank, or a foreign CRA with five years' experience (unverified list).
- **Capital:** the 1999 text set a minimum net worth of Rs 5 crore at registration. A 2018 amendment, effective 30 May 2018, raised this to Rs 25 crore, to be maintained at all times.
- **Ongoing obligations:** follow the code of conduct; run a rating committee and disclose rating methods; meet conflict-of-interest rules, including a 10% cap on cross-shareholding between CRAs; keep ratings under ongoing surveillance; hold a half-yearly internal audit (details beyond net worth and the cross-shareholding cap are unverified).
- **Sources:** https://www.sebi.gov.in/acts/CreditRatingAgencies.html, https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/oct-2017/1509259338723.pdf
